# The Last Curtain: full gamepad play — plan

**Status:** proposed, for the user's review (2026-11-25). Nothing is built yet.

**Goal:** the whole slice can be played with a PlayStation pad (any SDL pad works; the prompts show PlayStation buttons), from the title to Stage 1. Prompts
always show the device in use: pad glyphs while the pad is used, keys while the keyboard is.

**What exists:**
- **Engine input** (`Input.hpp`): SDL gamepad buttons act like keys; the sticks have a dead zone.
- **Keys** (`keys-keyboard.json` + `Keys`): `pad` names for back, confirm, next, end turn, skip,
  notebook, pause.
- **Board:** a cursor that steps by direction and repeats while held.
- **Other:** an on-screen keyboard (`Typing`) for the name; UI focus moves between buttons.

**What is missing:**
- glyph pictures;
- switching prompts by device;
- the café walk with the stick;
- the map lens with the stick;
- notebook navigation;
- battle picks;
- the inspect and choose panels from notes 7–9;
- camera zoom and pan with the pad;
- shoulder buttons for the order strip.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-25 02:51)

1. **Glyphs: PlayStation** (the user's pad is a PlayStation controller). Cross, Circle, Square,
   Triangle, L1/R1, L2/R2, the sticks, the D-pad, Create and Options. Xbox glyphs can come later
   from the same sheet layout.
2. **Camera: the right stick pans; L2/R2 zoom.**
3. **Rumble: a light rumble** on hits, falls and the breath, with a setting to turn it off.

In the tasks below the buttons are named by place, as SDL names them: south = Cross, east =
Circle, west = Square, north = Triangle.

## Tasks (in order; each one shippable alone)

1. **Last device used.** `Input` records whether a pad or the keyboard/mouse acted last.
   `Keys::Prompt(meaning)` returns the glyph or the key, and every prompt and hint asks it. A
   `{key:endTurn}` token in `localization.csv` text becomes the glyph or key when shown, so the
   words stay in the CSV.
2. **Glyph pictures.** One small sheet: Cross, Circle, Square, Triangle, L1 R1 L2 R2, the sticks
   (and stick clicks), the D-pad, Create, Options. They are drawn as images inside text with the UI's `{img:...}` inline images,
   or as a small image beside the label. Lacquer style, painted on the PC.
3. **The board.**
   - The left stick or D-pad moves the cursor; Cross picks; Circle goes back or hides the panel (note 7).
   - Triangle ends the turn; Square opens Kỹ năng.
   - L1/R1 step through the order strip, and the camera looks at each unit (note 8).
   - The cursor on a unit shows its card; Cross on another unit opens the inspect panel.
   - The pick-one panel (note 9) takes the D-pad.
   - The right stick pans and L2/R2 zoom (decision 2).
4. **The battle.** The picks are a vertical list moved by the D-pad; Cross picks; Circle is Rút khỏi trận,
   asking to confirm. The guided pick is focused first.
5. **The café and the map.**
   - The left stick walks in the café, and Cross talks or uses.
   - L1/R1 switch who walks (Tab today).
   - On the map, the stick moves the cursor over the tickets and the right stick pans the lens.
6. **Menus and the notebook.** Every screen has a first focus and a working back. In the notebook,
   L1/R1 switch tabs and the D-pad moves through the slots; Cross takes, Triangle drops. Settings and the
   keyboard are already done; they are checked again.
7. **Prompts audit.** A test walks every screen's words and fails on a hard-coded key name (like
   "F" or "Esc") outside `{key:...}`. This follows the localization test.
8. **Rumble (decision 3).** SDL rumble on a blow, a fall and the breath, scaled by strength, with a
   setting to turn it off.
9. **Check.** A run of the slice with only the pad on your PC, with your PlayStation pad. Note anything that needs the mouse.

**Out of scope:** remapping pad buttons in game (the file can remap), touch, and the Steam Deck.

**Risks:**
- Two ways to hide the panel (B and right click) must behave the same; they share `Back()`.
- The map lens and the café walk have mouse-only code paths today.

**How it's verified:**
- task 7's prompts test runs headless;
- the rest is your pad run on Windows (§4 of its testing record).
