## 0b. Bảo and Bà Mai: café characters, and the third tutorial's companions

The ten recruits are the official allies; **Bảo and Bà Mai are café characters** (the user). Their
stories still feed Le Thanh's. **They fight beside you only in the third tutorial** (the user), as Le
Thanh's help while you learn: biding, falls and reviving. They wear the khăn tang **loose around the
neck**, because they serve her by choice: a hint for the reveal.

### Bác sĩ Bảo — the House Doctor

- **From:** the old cast's Dinh Quoc Bao, the house's company doctor, who wrote false death papers.
- **Look:** about 50, a 1940s white doctor's coat over dark trousers, a stethoscope, round spectacles,
  a fountain pen in his breast pocket, a slim surgeon's scalpel.
- **Story:** on the night of the 1944 fire he signed Le Thanh's death paper as "killed in the air
  raid". He stays near her out of guilt. His secret is the first crack in her story. He knows it
  wasn't the raid, and he says so at the reveal.
- **Kit for T3 (Attacker line):** *Dao mổ* (scalpel): battle strike, 2 HP, power 6. *Chẩn đoán*
  (diagnosis): board hit, 3 HP, power 4, range 5 straight; the target takes +1 damage until its next
  turn. *Giấy chứng tử* (the death paper): passive; when an enemy falls to him, he heals 2.
- **Voice:** Saigon-educated 1940s Southern, formal, dry wit, avoids talking about that night.

### Bà Mai — the Caretaker

- **From:** the old cast's Phan Thi Mai, caretaker for forty years, who leaves rice by the trap door
  for her son under the stage.
- **Look:** about 65, a brown áo bà ba, a grey bun with a wooden pin, a bunch of keys at her belt, a
  small bowl of rice in her hands.
- **Story:** she kept the house through the dark years and died there. Her son is one of the
  lingering dead in the house; she doesn't know which. Finding him could be her own quiet thread.
- **Kit for T3 (Supporter line):** *tend*, *mend*, and *Cơm nguội* (cold rice): board, 2 HP,
  adjacent; heals 5 and cleanses one status. She revives in T3, where reviving is taught.
- **Voice:** Central-born, living in Saigon; warm, scolding, calls everyone "con".

**The rest of the old cast** (Khang, Lan, Huy, Nhi) is not in the campaign. The later quick match
uses the campaign's characters instead (the user).

## 1. The two heroes (power cards, honoured café guests)

Heroes are **never fought and never on strings** (the user). They come to the café once, as honoured
guests, and give a power card: **once per stage, every stage after** (the user). They are known **by
titles only** (the user). The art must be **stylized and respectful, not a likeness copied from a
photograph**, with the warm, dignified mood words.

### The Young Electrician of Công Lý (inspired by Nguyễn Văn Trỗi)

- **When:** after Stage 11 (the Tiger), before the patron's stage.
- **Look:** 24, a white short-sleeved work shirt, dark trousers, an electrician's tool pouch, calm
  steady eyes. He wears **no khăn tang**: he never forgot who he was. A warm gold rim.
- **Scene:** he sits at the café's best table and asks for nothing. He tells you about Saigon and the
  girl he loved, in a few short lines, and only historical facts. He is the one guest Le Thanh can't
  make forget, and she is visibly uneasy around him (a seed for the reveal).
- **Power card: Nhìn quê hương** ("let me see my homeland"). Once per stage: every fallen ally stands
  up at 1 HP, and every ally gains +2 Defence for the round.
- **Voice:** Quảng Nam accent living in Saigon; few words, steady.

### The Girl of Đất Đỏ (inspired by Võ Thị Sáu)

- **When:** after Stage 6 (the Goat, the stepmother who sold a child).
- **Look:** about 18, a plain áo bà ba, hair in a simple bun with a flower tucked in, barefoot. No
  khăn tang; a warm gold rim.
- **Scene:** she is the only guest who sings in the café, and Le Thanh stops to listen. Her song is
  written fresh, with no real lyrics. She leaves a flower on the counter.
- **Power card: Tiếng hát bình minh** ("the song at dawn"). Once per stage: every enemy, the zodiac
  included, loses its next action.
- **Voice:** Southern country girl; bright, unafraid, teases the player gently.

## 3. The ordinary ghosts

The lingering dead without a name: every class of person the war took (the user). They are **smaller,
softer and rounder** than the zodiac bosses: shorter, simpler shapes, no masks, no claws. Their eyes
are covered by the khăn tang of the rite. **Nature is shown by colour, class by costume** (the user).
There is one painting per class, and the engine tints the nature.

**Natures:**

| Nature | Colour | In the stage | In the café |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Friendly** | Soft green glow | Helps: stands still and heals an ally next to it once, or blocks a tile for you | Sometimes sits as a guest; one line each |
| **Neutral** | Pale blue-grey | Wanders and blocks tiles. **A zodiac can string it**: it turns red and hostile (a stage gimmick) | Watches Le Thanh dance |
| **Aggressive** | Red glow, **red strings** from above | The zodiac's soldiers: fights you | Never; red ghosts can't enter the café |

Allies (joined recruits) glow **blue** (the user), a clearer blue than the neutral blue-grey, and
carry a name plate.

**Classes** (one costume each):

| Class | Costume (who they were) | Battle behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Attacker** | A soldier in a faded uniform and pith helmet, a rifle held low | Strikes hardest |
| **Defender** | A farmer-militiawoman with a conical hat and a woven bamboo shield | Shields, slow |
| **Supporter** | A nurse with a red-cross armband and a cloth bag (the user's example) | Heals others |
| **Speedster** | A child in an oversized shirt, barefoot, a paper message | Fast, weak |
| **All-Rounder** | A student in a white shirt with a satchel | Balanced |

**Mix of classes [call]:** the generic ghosts reuse the five archetypes for every class of people. The
named recruits carry the specific stories.

**Stats [call]:** weaker than the recruits' class lines (about 60%), so they read as minions.

## 5. Le Thanh, the hostess and final boss

**Her forms:**

| Form | When | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **The hostess** (the user) | The whole campaign | 25, a **red bridal áo dài**, a hair ribbon, silver bracelets (`cast.md`). Gentle, formal, never raises her voice. A khăn tang? **No:** she is the one who ties them. Her eyes are just a little too still. |
| **Phase 1: The Conductor** | Final stage, first half | The same dress, now with **red strings running from her fingers** to every specter in the room. She stands on the stage and does not move. |
| **Phase 2: The Bride in Embers** | Final stage, second half | The Opera Specter from `cast.md`: the red dress turned to embers, the ribbon a line of smoke, the bracelets glowing. She bows to each of you before she sings. Larger than any zodiac. |

**The fight [call]:**
- **Phase 1, the audience.** Le Thanh can't be hit. Every lingering spirit whose memory you didn't
  restore fights for her as a red ghost. Every one you restored stands beside you as an ally outside
  your five. **Cutting her strings:** each red ghost you free cuts one string. When all are cut, phase 2 begins.
- **Phase 2, the rite.** Le Thanh is a boss with the Supporter-Attacker kit:
  - **Khúc ca lãng quên** (the song of forgetting): each round one of your units forgets its abilities
    for 2 turns (silence).
  - **Lửa cưới** (the wedding fire): fire within 2 tiles of her, spreading (from `cast.md`'s Flame curse).
  - **Cúi chào** (the bow): before a big attack she bows to its target, a fair warning that's part of
    her character, not intent shown by the UI.
  - **Breaking the song:** a restored recruit next to a silenced ally can spend its action to speak a
    memory (a line worded by the model from its own fragments), which lifts the silence. The memories
    you restored are your weapon against forgetting.
- **HP:** about 200 over phase 2 [call, soak to tune].
- **The ending.** Win, and she is pacified: she remembers who she was before she became a star, and the
  spirits move on. The epilogue shows each restored spirit leaving, one line each. Lose, and the
  credits roll over a full house watching her sing, you among them, a khăn tang over your eyes (the
  bad ending). The player falling in this fight is a loss like any other stage: retry, café or quit.
